The sort is far slower than the other three jobs, so parallel time ~ sort time:
best possible speedup = serial total / slowest section = 1.02x, whatever the team size.
To do better, split the big job: e.g. sort the two halves in two sections and merge,
or parallelise the sort itself with tasks.
