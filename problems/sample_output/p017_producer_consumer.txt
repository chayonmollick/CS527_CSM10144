Why not  while (cnt == B) ;  with a plain read? With no synchronisation inside the
loop the compiler may load cnt ONCE into a register and spin forever, and the CPU may
show this thread a stale buffer slot. atomic read forces a fresh read of cnt each
time; flush orders 'write item' before 'count++' and 'see count' before 'read item'.
