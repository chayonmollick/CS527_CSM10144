% dot_part.txt - one task's share of a PARALLEL DOT PRODUCT  A[0]*B[0] + ... + A[31]*B[31]
% Task:   A and B are split into 4 chunks of 8; task k gets A's chunk k and B's chunk k
%         in its own data file, computes their dot product and leaves it at [4]. The
%         dot product of the whole vectors is the sum of the 4 partial results.
% Data:   N at 0; the result word at 4; A's chunk at 8 .. 4N + 4; B's chunk right after
%         it, at 4N + 8 .. 8N + 4.   dot1..dot4.byte: N = 8 each
% Result: the partial dot product at 4 (also printed to log.txt)
%         dot1..dot4 -> 164, 61, -22, -56; A.B = their sum = 147
% Method: scalar counted loop with two pointers that move together (as in
%         ../../minicomputer/programs/dot_product.txt).
% Regs:   x1 N, x2 i, x3 &A[i], x4 &B[i], x5 A[i] (then the product), x6 B[i], x10 sum
x1 = [0]          % N
x2 = 0            % i
x3 = 8            % &A[0]
x4 = x1 * 4
x4 = x4 + 8       % &B[0] = 8 + 4N
x10 = 0           % partial dot product
.loop
x9 = x2 - x1
BGE .done         % i >= N
x5 = [x3]         % A[i]
x6 = [x4]         % B[i]
x5 = x5 * x6
x10 = x10 + x5
x3 = x3 + 4
x4 = x4 + 4
x2 = x2 + 1
BAL .loop
.done
[4] = x10         % partial result at 4
print x10
